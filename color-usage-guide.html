Umodzi Impact

Colour usage

Same brand hexes. Orange leads the mood (funny, energetic, youthful) via complementary contrast with teal rests — not navy slabs. Change volume and roles, not the palette.

1. Orange

30%

#F18B05 · Energy, humour & action

Full orange fields only on home hero and celebration CTAs. Elsewhere: warm wash grounds with orange stamps, tape, scribbles, and primary buttons. Navy type on orange fills. Not for long body text on white (~2.5:1).

2. Green

24%

#396831 · Outdoor / alive

Field energy, nature cues, playful section washes, values — use more widely than one sustainability block.

3. Royal Blue

24%

#314172 · Structure & type only

Headings, body text, punch words on orange fields. Avoid large navy background bands — they read as a serious brochure.

4. Teal / Blue

22%

#26546B · Cool complementary rest

Active navigation, focus, quiet section pauses after orange heat. Keeps orange feeling brighter by contrast — not large cool slabs.

Header and footer stay white so the teal logo remains clear. Soft washes (orange / green / teal tints) are derived surfaces for scrapbook fills — not new brand colours. Mist is warm peach-tinted, not cool grey. Logo green file is #39792F; interface green uses the swatch above.
